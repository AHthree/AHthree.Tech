What this service is designed to solve

Setting up in Saudi Arabia can involve several authorities, documents, licenses and professional decisions. AHthree.tech helps coordinate the journey and connect you with the appropriate licensed specialists where regulated work is required.

  • Commercial Registration coordination — support with the setup path and required information.
  • Licensing & activity setup — identify the business activities and licensing steps relevant to the intended structure.
  • Visa-related guidance — practical coordination around business and individual visa matters where applicable.
  • Compliance coordination — keep operational requirements visible as the business grows.
  • Management support — help organize the next operational steps after the initial setup.

Frequently asked questions

Do you provide legal advice?

We provide business setup coordination and practical guidance. Regulated legal, accounting or government services are handled with appropriately licensed professionals where required.

Can every foreign investor use the same setup route?

No. The correct route depends on ownership, activity, structure and current regulations. We assess the situation before recommending a path.

Can you guarantee approval?

No. Government approvals and licensing decisions remain with the relevant authorities.
